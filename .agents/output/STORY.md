# USA Flag Nail Art Step-by-Step Tutorial

This short-form video tutorial demonstrates a 3-step process for creating a realistic USA flag nail design on an elegant lady's hand.

**Scene 1 (The Stripes):** 
The tutorial focuses on the foundational stripes. The nail features the deep red and crisp white stripes. A fine brush sweeps carefully over the lower section of the nail, perfecting the lines of the flag.

**Scene 2 (The Stars):** 
The next step details the starry canton. With the navy blue section in place, a tiny dotting tool hovers over the blue area, pressing gently to refine the delicate white stars.

**Scene 3 (The Final Reveal):**
The fully cured, ultra-glossy USA flag nail is shown in its completed state. The finger performs a slow, deliberate tilt, allowing the light to catch the flawless, patriotic design.

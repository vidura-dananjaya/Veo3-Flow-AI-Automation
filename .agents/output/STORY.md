# A Dog's Memory Lasts Forever (Viral Facebook Reel Video Script)

**Scenes Count:** 5
**Target Audience:** European countries
**Vibe:** Highly emotional, heartwarming, deeply touching, 100% realistic, viral

## Scene 1: The Beginning of a Beautiful Friendship
In the cozy front yard of a rustic European grandmother's cottage, an 8-year-old young boy with messy light-brown hair and wearing a red and white striped t-shirt is happily playing. He is rolling in the green grass with an incredibly cute, fluffy, golden-colored Golden Retriever puppy. They are both full of joy, establishing an instant, unbreakable bond.

## Scene 2: The Unbreakable Bond
Inside the cozy cottage, the same 8-year-old boy (with messy light-brown hair, wearing the red and white striped t-shirt) is fast asleep on a warm, thick woven rug. Curled up tightly right next to him is the same fluffy, golden-colored Golden Retriever puppy. The puppy rests its tiny head on the boy's chest, showing the deepest level of comfort and friendship.

## Scene 3: The Heartbreaking Goodbye
It is a rainy, gloomy day. The same 8-year-old boy (with messy light-brown hair, wearing the red and white striped t-shirt) is sitting inside the back seat of a car, waving sadly out the window. Sitting on the porch of the rustic cottage is the same fluffy, golden-colored Golden Retriever puppy, looking incredibly heartbroken and sad as it watches its best friend leave. 

## Scene 4: The Return After Ten Years
Ten years have passed. It is a bright, sunny afternoon. An 18-year-old young man with exactly the same messy light-brown hair, now wearing a simple white t-shirt and blue jeans, walks up to the familiar gate of the rustic European grandmother's cottage. Sitting patiently by the porch is a large, majestic adult Golden Retriever dog with the same beautiful golden-colored fur. 

## Scene 5: True Love Never Fades
The large, majestic adult Golden Retriever instantly recognizes its childhood friend. The dog runs up to the 18-year-old young man (with messy light-brown hair) and joyfully jumps up, wrapping its front paws around him in a tight, highly emotional hug. The young man has tears of pure joy in his eyes as he hugs the dog back tightly. It is a beautiful moment proving that a dog's love and memory last forever.
